Your privacy

ClauseWatch reads terms and privacy pages on this device. There is no account. Apart from the daily rules check, nothing leaves it unless you turn on AI help with your own key.

  • No telemetry, no browsing history, no usage data, no analytics.
  • The daily rules check downloads the latest signed rules from the project's GitHub releases. It sends no ID and nothing about you or the sites you visit; GitHub sees an ordinary download.
  • Reading a service's terms fetches those public pages from the service itself, without your cookies.
  • AI help with your own key sends the service you chose what you ask about: the text being checked (the document, or the text you pasted or selected, up to 24,000 characters) when you ask it to look for clauses the rules missed, or only the quotes found for a summary. The browser's built-in model runs on this device.
