Set up ClauseWatch

ClauseWatch quotes what a service's terms and privacy policy say, before you sign up. Never a score, never "safe". Quotes from the documents, not legal advice.

Two choices, about a minute. You can change both later in Settings. ClauseWatch collects nothing about you: no telemetry, no browsing history.

Do you want AI help?

ClauseWatch always works without AI: its rules find the clauses and quote them. AI can add a short plain-English summary.

AI help
You can change this any time in Settings → AI help.

When should ClauseWatch appear?

When ClauseWatch appears

Seeing it too often on a site you use every day? Choose "Don't show on this site" on the card. Any time, right-click a terms link or selected text and choose ClauseWatch.

Nothing to switch on for counting

ClauseWatch never sends usage data. Its only regular request is the daily download of the signed rules from the project's GitHub releases, which carries no ID.
